Prospective Students

I am actively seeking research assistants to work on AI agents, with focus areas including memory systems, agent orchestration, and agent safety. Remote positions are also available.

Who I’m looking for:

  • Impact-driven mindset: You care about building systems that matter and solve real problems
  • Attention to detail: You sweat the small things because details compound into excellence
  • Hacker spirit: You love diving deep, breaking things to understand them, and building from first principles

If this resonates with you, please reach out via email (angelayang@sjtu.edu.cn) with:

  • Your CV or background
  • A brief note on what excites you about AI agents
  • Links to code/projects you’re proud of (GitHub, papers, documents, etc.)

I welcome students at all levels - undergraduate, graduate, or people who are seeking post-doctoral positions.
