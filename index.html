About Me

My background can be summarized as: 7 years of Academia Training + 7 years at Meta Ads Core ML = A Researcher with Strong Engineering Ability and Impact-driven Mindset

My academic journey began with a focus on complex systems and network science, where I explored both ends of the research spectrum—from highly theoretical work, such as analyzing stability in dynamical systems using Lyapunov functions (published in Physical Review Letters), to large-scale, data-driven studies of real-world infrastructure, including modeling the dynamics of the entire U.S. power transmission network (published in Science). This experience shaped my research taste: I am particularly drawn to work that either uncovers fundamental insights or demonstrates real-world impact at scale.

When I worked as a Senior Staff Research Scientist (E7) at Meta, I continued to pursue this philosophy in a production environment. From 2023 to 2025, I was the technical lead for the Business AI Agent, leading the effort from 0 to 1 and successfully launching it to support millions of small businesses worldwide. Prior to that, between 2018 and 2022, I worked on the core Ads machine learning team, where I led efforts in privacy-preserving machine learning, developing models that balance performance with strict privacy constraints. My story was featured by the SIAM community.

Across both academia and industry, these experiences have shaped how I approach research: I care deeply about clarity of ideas, strength of execution, and meaningful impact. I am particularly interested in building systems that are not only publishable, but also deployable—systems that can stand the test of real-world complexity while contributing new understanding to the field.

Lab Culture

Having spent most of my professional career at Meta (formerly Facebook), its culture has left a mark on how I think about research and collaboration. I strongly resonate with several core principles:

  • Move Fast and Fail Fast: In a fast-moving AI landscape, quickly testing and discarding weak ideas accelerates learning and ensures we focus on what truly matters.
  • Focus on Long-Term Impact: Research should be evaluated not only by publications, but by its potential to be adopted in real-world systems and create meaningful benefits.
  • Build Awesome Things: I place strong emphasis on engineering quality and strive to build systems that are both elegant and practically useful, grounded in real needs.
  • Be Direct and Respect Your Colleagues: I value clear, honest communication while maintaining mutual respect—this defines how I collaborate with others.
